# Shima privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-18

## The short version

Shima has no account, no analytics and no ads, and there is no Shima server. What the island shows is read on your phone and used there. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Notification access

The notification listener is how the island knows what is playing and picks up live updates from other apps, like a timer, a ride or a delivery. Those notifications are read in memory to draw the island. Shima does not save them or send them anywhere.

## Accessibility service

The accessibility service lets the island draw above the status bar and carries out the gestures you set on it: back, home, recent apps, the notification shade, quick settings, lock screen and screenshot. It also checks which app is open, so the island can stay out of the way of the apps you choose. It does not read what is on your screen.

## Music, artwork and lyrics

The app that is playing supplies the title, artist, artwork and, if it offers them, lyrics. When a player gives a web link for its artwork instead of the image itself, Shima downloads that one image from the player's link. Artwork is held in memory for the current track and never saved.

## Alerts that ask for a permission

A few alerts need a permission of their own, asked for only when you turn that alert on. Bluetooth tells the island which headset connected and its battery. Location is what Android asks of an app that reads the name of the Wi-Fi network you joined or notices an app starting GPS; Shima never reads where you are. The screenshot alert asks you to pick your Screenshots folder, and reads only that folder, to show the shot you just took. The microphone and camera alerts need no permission: they learn only that something is using them, never what it hears or sees.

## Clipboard

Shima reads the clipboard only when you use the Translate clipboard gesture, and hands the text straight to the translation app you pick. It does not keep a copy.

## Purchases

Pro and tips are paid through Google Play, which handles the payment under Google's own privacy policy. Shima receives Play's signed receipt and keeps it on your phone to unlock Pro; it never sees your payment details. Update checks and the rating prompt go through Google Play too.

## Backups

A backup is a file you make, by hand or on a schedule you set, saved in the Shima folder in Downloads. It holds your settings and nothing else, and Shima never uploads it. If Android's own backup is on, your phone may include Shima's settings in the copy it keeps in your Google account, as it does for other apps.

## Bug reports and email

Report a bug opens your email app with the app version, phone model and Android version filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it, and you can change or delete any of it first. Emails are used only to reply to you.

## What stays on your phone

Your settings, the apps you have opened from search, and a log of when the island's services started and stopped are kept in Shima's private storage. While the App uninstalled alert is on, so is the name, icon, version, size and source of each app you have installed, because Android stops saying what an app was the moment it is uninstalled. That list is never part of a backup, and turning the alert off deletes it. Uninstalling Shima or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

## This policy

The app fetches this policy from GitHub when you open it, so GitHub sees an ordinary web request from your phone. If it cannot be reached, the copy that came with the app is shown instead.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and the release notes say what is different.
